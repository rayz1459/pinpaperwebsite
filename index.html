How it works

From a board to a desktop
full of wallpapers.

Pin Paper works like a canvas. You link your boards, lay out image frames, shape them, and let it build the set. Here's the whole flow, start to finish.

Link a board or a folder

Open Sources and choose Add Source. Paste a Pinterest board link or pick a folder on your computer, and Pin Paper pulls in every image as a pool you can use. Add as many sources as you want; they stack up in your library.

Pin Paper imports the whole board or folder at once. No saving images one by one.

Lay out frames on the canvas

Add a frame for each image you want, then drag the frames into the arrangement you like. Drop a single pin into a frame, or assign a whole board and let Pin Paper fill it.

Move, resize, and reorder frames freely. The layout is yours to shape.

Customize each frame

This is what makes a wallpaper feel like a board, not a screenshot. Turn flat images into pinned-up prints: Polaroid frames or torn-paper edges, effects, and soft drop shadows.

Polaroid, torn paper, textures, and shadows, all set per photo.

Preview, then create the set

Try the layout right on your desktop, then choose Create Wallpaper Set. Pin Paper renders a set from your boards and cycles through it on your desktop. Every wallpaper is different, none repeating, sized for your screen.

Save the layout as a template and regenerate a fresh set anytime.
